*Abstract*: 
In this work-in-progress talk, I will attempt to clarify some of the issues
surrounding second- and higher-order logics. How should we understand the
relationship between the proof-theoretic inferentialist presentations of such
logics and their model-theoretic truth conditional semantics? I will argue that
in an important sense, the so-called full semantics for second- and
higher-order logics is too strong, and that a wider class of models is not only
necessary, but is  mathematically and philosophically desirable.  The good
news: such a class of models already exists and is well understood. (And no,
it’s just not the Henkin semantics.)
